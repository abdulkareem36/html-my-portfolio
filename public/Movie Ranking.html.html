The Five Best Movies of Actor Vijay

1. Theri (2016)

Theri is a 2016 Indian Tamil-language action thriller film written and directed by Atlee Kumar.
The film stars Vijay in dual roles, alongside Samantha Ruth Prabhu and Amy Jackson.
The story revolves around a former police officer who goes into hiding to protect his daughter from a vengeful gangster.

2. Mersal (2017)

Mersal is a 2017 Indian Tamil-language action thriller film directed by Atlee Kumar.
The film features Vijay in triple roles, alongside Samantha Ruth Prabhu, Nithya Menen,
and Kajal Aggarwal. The plot follows a doctor who seeks justice for his family while exposing corruption in the medical field.

3. Master (2021)

Master is a 2021 Indian Tamil-language action thriller film directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj.
The film stars Vijay as a charismatic professor who is sent to a juvenile school to reform the students but ends up confronting a ruthless gangster played by Vijay Sethupathi. The movie explores themes of redemption and justice.

4. Gilli (2004)

Gilli is a 2004 Indian Tamil-language action thriller film directed by Dharani.
The film stars Vijay in the lead role, alongside Trisha Krishnan and Prakash Raj.
The story follows a young man who becomes a skilled kabaddi player and faces challenges in his personal and professional life, ultimately striving to achieve his dreams.

5. Thuppaki (2012)

Thuppaki is a 2012 Indian Tamil-language action thriller film directed by AR Murugadoss.
The film features Vijay in the lead role, alongside Kajal Aggarwal and Vidyut Jammwal.
The plot revolves around an army officer who uncovers a terrorist plot and takes on the responsibility of protecting innocent lives while facing personal dilemmas.
